# Limitations — what this kit is not

Stated plainly, so you can judge the fit before you buy:

- **No networking, no multiplayer, no co-op.** Single-player only, by deliberate scope.
- **The art set is one locked set, not an art library.** It is the game's coherent presentation — towers, enemies, maps, interface — minimal where it can be, and fully reskinnable. No separate model or sprite packs are included or sold.
- **No custom rendering.** No custom shaders, no Shader Graph systems, no VFX packs.
- **Generation and direction are systems, not content infinity.** Seeded maps vary measurably and waves are composed to budgets — but the campaign, tower roster and enemy roster in the box are the recorded, finite content listed in the verification record.
- **One product, one edition.** There is no second tier or add-on module at launch; what is documented here is what ships.
