# The Campaign & Sample Game

The kit ships as a playable game, not a parts bin:

- **A campaign of maps** with progression — clear a map to unlock the next — difficulty steps across the campaign, and meta systems (tower unlocks, permanent upgrades) that persist between runs.
- **A full loop on every map**: place towers → directed waves → earn and spend → upgrade or sell → clear or leak your last life. Each map triggers its win and lose conditions in scripted verification runs.
- **One locked art set** — towers, enemies, map props and interface art as one coherent stylised set, frozen before the presentation leg and judged against a fixed quality benchmark before listing. The verification record lists every art asset shipped, with its source.
- **Package content only** — no external asset dependency, no missing-reference errors on import.

Play the campaign first. Then open the data files behind the map you just cleared — every tower, wave and economy number you met is a file you can read and change.
