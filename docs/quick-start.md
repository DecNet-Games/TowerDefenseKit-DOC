# Quick Start — a map, a tower, a directed wave

1. **Generate your first map.** Enter any seed: the kit produces the grid, the path, the buildable and blocked cells, entry and exit. Re-enter the same seed — the same map. Change the seed — a new map, still with a guaranteed valid path.
2. **Place your first tower.** Pick a buildable cell; the kit refuses path cells, blocked cells and placements that would block the only remaining route — with a clear reason, not a silent failure.
3. **Survive your first directed wave.** The director composes the wave from its budget under the difficulty curve. Your tower fires under its targeting mode; kills pay currency; a leaked enemy costs a life.
4. **Upgrade and sell.** Upgrade the tower — the stat change and cost are exactly the documented amounts. Sell it — the documented fraction comes back and the cell frees up.
5. **Open the campaign screen.** Clear the map and the next unlocks; your meta upgrades persist into the next run.

From here: [Grid & Paths]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/features/grid-and-paths/) and [Towers & Upgrades]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/features/towers-and-upgrades/) document the data formats, and [Generated Maps]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/features/generated-maps/) explains the seed contract.
